## Statement of Applicability (SoA)

The SoA for Ishastra includes all Annex A controls except:
- A.6.1, A.6.4, A.6.5 (HR/people - organization responsibility)
- A.7.1-A.7.13 (Physical - cloud provider responsibility)
- A.8.1 (User endpoints - not in scope)

Justification documented in `compliance/soa.md`.
